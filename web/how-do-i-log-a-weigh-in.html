How do I log a weigh-in?

You enter the weight. DoseTrace keeps the weigh-in with the earlier ones you logged. It does not set a goal for you, and it does not interpret the change.

Example only

One Reel showed a weigh-in saved as 81.4 kg, after a prior figure around 81.6 kg, and a change of about 0.6 kg down since 30 October. A daily-burn figure on that demo was around 2,090 kcal. Those numbers belong to the Reel.

A separate demo Progress screen, below, shows different numbers, including 84.1 kg. It is not the same example. The screen can also show a target and a range that were calculated from what that demo entered. DoseTrace does not turn that into advice.

DoseTrace calculates with the numbers you type. It does not choose a dose, does not interpret a lab result, and does not check drug interactions. It is for your own records, not a clinical tool for treating patients.
