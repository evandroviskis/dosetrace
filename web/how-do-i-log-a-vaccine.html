How do I log a vaccine?

You enter the name, the date, and the notes you want to keep. DoseTrace stores that record. It does not advise which vaccine to get, or when.

Example only

A Reel’s demo log included a flu shot and a Tdap booster, with a date around 6 November 2026. Those names are the demo. They are not a schedule for you.

DoseTrace calculates with the numbers you type. It does not choose a dose, does not interpret a lab result, and does not check drug interactions. It is for your own records, not a clinical tool for treating patients.
