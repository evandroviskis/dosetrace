How does DoseTrace chart a lab marker over time?

You keep lab results by marker. DoseTrace charts that marker over time so you can see the numbers you entered, next to each other.

It organizes the values. It does not interpret them, compare them with a diagnosis, or tell you what to do next.

Example only

A DoseTrace Reel showed one marker, Vitamin D, as 24, then 31, then 36 ng/mL. Those three numbers are an example from that Reel. They are not a reading of your labs, and the rise is not a conclusion.

The pictures are title cards from the Reel, not a chart of those values. The written steps are on how a lab marker is charted.

DoseTrace calculates with the numbers you type. It does not choose a dose, does not interpret a lab result, and does not check drug interactions. It is for your own records, not a clinical tool for treating patients.
