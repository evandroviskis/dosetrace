How is half of a tablet calculated?

Divide the amount you type by the amount printed on one tablet. The result is a fraction of that labeled amount. It is not a decision to split the tablet.

Example only

A Reel used 800 mg and a tablet labeled 1,600 mg. 800 ÷ 1,600 = 1/2. A tablet labeled 1,600 mg is not a real product here.

This is arithmetic only. It does not mean this tablet, or any particular tablet, is suitable for splitting. Check with your prescriber or pharmacist. Do not split extended-release, enteric-coated, or other tablets that are not suitable for splitting.

The same arithmetic is on the tablet fraction calculator.

DoseTrace calculates with the numbers you type. It does not choose a dose, does not interpret a lab result, and does not check drug interactions. It is for your own records, not a clinical tool for treating patients.
