How does DoseTrace keep a dose history?

You mark a dose complete or skipped. A missed dose can remain in the history until you correct it. DoseTrace keeps that record, including adherence for today, the last 7 days, and the last 30 days.

The history is yours. Premium includes a PDF export of records you choose to share with your own clinician. DoseTrace does not treat a patient, and it does not tell a clinician what to do.
