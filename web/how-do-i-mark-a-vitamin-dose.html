How do I mark a vitamin dose?

You mark the dose you already take. The history can show that mark as complete, skipped, or missed. DoseTrace does not choose the vitamin, and it does not choose the amount.

The pictures are title cards. They do not name a product.

Reminders for a dose you already plan are on how reminders work. The record of what you marked is on dose history.

DoseTrace calculates with the numbers you type. It does not choose a dose, does not interpret a lab result, and does not check drug interactions. It is for your own records, not a clinical tool for treating patients.
