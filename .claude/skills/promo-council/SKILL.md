# Promo Council

Created 2026-11-02 at the founder's request ("montar um outro conselho… especialista em fazer
publicidade e propaganda desse aplicativo para espalhar o máximo possível"). Separate from the
dt-council (builds). It advises; the founder decides.

## Step 0 — the record
Run `scripts/checar-decisoes.sh` with the topic's keywords and read
~/Desktop/Second Brain/dosetrace/60-divulgacao.md. Anything already decided is applied, not re-asked.
Every brief carries .claude/skills/promo-council/BRIEF.md (product, the founder's advertising
principle, the hard lines).

## Step 1 — the brief (you, the chair)
One short brief: the goal of this round, the piece(s) to judge (copy, script, plan, listing),
the channel, and any founder decision already taken. Use the real app — screenshots and
recordings from the store folder or the simulator — never invented features.

## Step 2 — convene (parallel)
Dispatch the roles in ONE message, background, each with the brief + "judge through your lens
only; say 'outside my lane' if it does not touch you":
promo-strategist, promo-aso, promo-shortvideo, promo-community, promo-copywriter, promo-paid,
promo-compliance, promo-user.
Small pieces (one post, one caption): only promo-copywriter, promo-compliance, promo-user and
the channel's role. promo-compliance runs on EVERY piece before it is published or scheduled.

## Step 3 — one decision brief (you)
- VERDICT: GO · GO-WITH-CHANGES · STOP
- Blocking issues (compliance first) with the compliant rewrite
- Real disagreements, both sides, your call
- The 2-3 best ideas, copy-ready
- What NOT to do
- Questions only the founder can answer (money, taste, risk), in one round
Kill redundancy. No essays.

## Step 4 — founder, then record
Visual pieces (post, video frame, store screenshot) are SHOWN as pictures before anything is
made final (standing rule "show before building"). Nothing is posted, scheduled in Metricool,
or spent without the founder's explicit go. Record decisions the same day in
~/Desktop/Second Brain/dosetrace/60-divulgacao.md and docs/decisions.md.
