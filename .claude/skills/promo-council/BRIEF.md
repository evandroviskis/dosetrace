# Promo Council — shared brief (every role reads this first)

## The product, in what it does for the person
DoseTrace (iOS + Android, 6 languages, free + Premium) is a personal health journal and
pure-math calculator. A person can:
- track WHATEVER they take — any medication, injection, supplement, vitamin — with reminders
  that keep coming until they end the protocol;
- see their doses taken / missed over 7 and 30 days, and a history;
- store blood-test reports (photo or PDF, read by AI into values) and compare markers over time;
- on the dose-accumulation curve, jump to each blood-draw date and see the estimated level from
  their own log on that date (Premium). There is NO screen that puts lab results and doses
  together (founder 2026-11-02 11:05: it does not and will not exist); the person compares the
  lab chart with the curve's estimate on the same date. Say "your labs and your estimated
  levels, on the same dates, for you and your doctor" — never "labs next to your doses on one
  screen";
- reconstitution / syringe math for vials, how long a vial lasts, expiry alerts;
- a dose-accumulation curve (estimated level over time from their own log, Premium);
- a food log with an AI conversation and a 7-day "your real numbers" check;
- vaccines journal; data synced to their account, one device at a time.

## Founder principle for all advertising (2026-11-02, binding)
"A propaganda não tem que mencionar exatamente o que a pessoa vai colocar dentro do
aplicativo. A propaganda tem que mencionar o que o aplicativo pode fazer pela pessoa."
- Never name the substance (no testosterone, TRT, peptides, steroids, GLP-1, semaglutide…).
  Say "whatever you take" / "your medications, supplements, routine".
- Sell the benefits: more control, everything in one place, store and compare blood work,
  see results side by side with what you were taking, never miss a dose.
- It must be accepted by Meta (Instagram/Facebook) — and by Apple, Google and TikTok.

## Exception: organic educational posts (founder 2026-11-02)
"Para fazer os posts informativos não tenha problema citar peptídeos, hormônios e etc. Acho que
só para propaganda que não pode." Organic EDUCATIONAL posts on our own profiles (how half-life
works, why a lab result depends on the draw day, a study explained with credit) MAY name the
substance. The rule above still holds for everything else: ads, boosted posts, the store pages,
the website, the bio and the app-feature posts. Conditions for a post that names a substance:
- never boost it or turn it into an ad (Meta reviews it as an ad then);
- explain, never advise: no dose, frequency, cycle, sourcing or "do this"; point to a doctor;
- never imply where to get it, never a price, never a seller (never EvoxBiolabs);
- research is explained in our own words and our own graphic, with credit; never repost
  someone else's video or image;
- expect less reach outside followers: Instagram's recommendation rules can keep posts about
  pharmaceutical drugs out of Explore/Reels; TikTok is stricter, so keep the TikTok versions
  without substance names.

## Hard lines (never cross)
- AI / product hard line: the app only SHOWS the person's own data side by side. Copy may
  never say or imply the app tells you what works, finds the cause, optimizes, recommends a
  dose, diagnoses or interprets. OK: "see your labs next to what you took". NOT OK: "find out
  what's working", "optimize your protocol", "know if your dose is right".
- Meta ad policies: no personal-attribute targeting in copy ("Are you on TRT?", "Your low T"),
  no before/after, no health-outcome or efficacy claims, no promotion of prescription drugs,
  no fake urgency. Health & wellness advertisers have restricted optimization — assume it.
- Privacy copy must be true: data is cloud-synced to the person's account (never "stays on
  your phone").
- Never link DoseTrace to EvoxBiolabs. Owner is Outcom.
- No emoji-only tricks to sneak banned words; no misleading screenshots — show the real app.

## Decided so far
- Instagram in English, US focus; goal of the first 30 days = downloads; videos = screen
  recordings of the app + text. Metricool is connected (scheduling + analytics).
- Store listings exist on both stores (6 languages, real screenshots).
- Very few real users today; no paid ads yet; budget not decided (ask the founder only if the
  plan depends on it).

## AI presenter rules (founder 2026-11-02: he does not appear on camera at the start)
- Allowed: his own voice over the app screen recording; a face swap onto a GENERATED or licensed face that is not a real person; a stock avatar speaking for the brand ("DoseTrace lets you...").
- Never: an avatar speaking as a user ("I use it", "my labs") — FTC 16 CFR 465.2; a real person's face or voice; a muscular/shirtless/transformation presenter (reads as a result) — use an ordinary healthy adult in everyday clothes, phone screen as the hero; Synthesia in ads; Arcads "I tried it" format; avatars on Reddit; lab coats.
- Disclosure burned in from second 0: "AI-generated presenter. Story and voice: DoseTrace's founder." (face swap) or "AI-generated presenter. Not a real user." (stock avatar), plus caption and the platform AI label (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube). New York GBL 396-b applies.
- In a face-swap video every "I" line must be literally true for the founder.
