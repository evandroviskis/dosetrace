# DoseTrace — divergence and gap register

- Compiled 2026-10-12 from the 8 dossiers in `docs/product/research/` (read in full), `docs/decisions.md`, `docs/specs/*.md` and `docs/review/features.md`. Branch `redesign/graduated`. No code was changed.
- **This is the one register of defects** for the PRD, the TRD and the flows. Reconciled 2026-10-12: the TRD-only findings (sync holes H-1…H-12, failure modes F-1…F-12, repo ↔ live drift DR-1…DR-9, risks R-01…R-50) were merged as DV-182…DV-189 or folded into existing rows (DV-001, DV-011, DV-088, DV-119); the PRD's gaps G-1…G-137 point to DV rows (PRD §6, DV column); every flow's "Divergence today" row (`Dnn-k`) is listed in the Sources of its DV row and each flow's §11 table carries the DV ID. Crosswalk at the end.
- Every divergence (D-x) and gap (G-x) of every dossier is here once. When two dossiers found the same issue, it is ONE row that lists every source ID. A few edge-case findings without a D/G number are included when they have a clear user impact; they are cited by section (e.g. `02:§7.3`).
- Source ID format: `NN:ID` where NN is the dossier (01 protocols, 02 doses/Today, 03 reminders, 04 food/reality check/Progress, 05 curve/Journey, 06 My Body, 07 onboarding/account/Premium, 08 platform). The dossiers use both `D6` and `D-6` styles; they are quoted as written.
- Decisions referenced in the "Founder decision" column live in `docs/product/OPEN-DECISIONS.md`:
  - `OPEN-xx` = a real question for the founder (taste, money, risk, scope). Do not build until answered.
  - `AD-xx` = already decided by the ledger or a signed spec (citation in OPEN-DECISIONS). Build to it.
  - `DL-xx` = decided by product logic (one-line reason in OPEN-DECISIONS). Build to it; the founder may override.
  - `No (bug)` = plain defect, no product choice involved.
- Every fix follows the evolution rules: the test in the last column is written first and must fail on today's code, then the fix makes it pass; the full suite stays green.
