# Unrecorded period beyond 14 days: "Você tomou?" (option B)

- source: founder 2026-11-03 22:14 EST ("B"), docs/decisions.md "Unrecorded period beyond 14 days: option B". One question per protocol for every scheduled dose with no record older than the 14-day Missed window, up to 1 year (the curve lookback): "De {de} a {até} há {n} doses de {nome} sem registro. Você tomou?" → Tomei todas no horário / Escolher quais / Não tomei. Two triggers: return to the app (Today) and right after saving a lab result with a past date. Reasons: precision of the estimated level, and "fiel ao que a pessoa diz que usou" (never assume taken; always ask).
- picture: docs/design/unrecorded-period.html (app today | proposal, the "Escolher quais" list, Dose log and curve after each answer, the lab moment, "Não lembro" (section 1), the lab line (section 4b), app-written vs person-marked "Perdida" (section 5); light and dark). Approved with the founder's answers of 2026-11-04 (his "B, pode testar e publicar a correção" of 2026-11-03 + "1 sim 2 sim 3 sim se foram ativamente marcadas como perdidas", 2026-11-04 10:04 EST).
- reuses: the dose-correction sheet (screens/components/DoseFixSheet.js, option A, docs/specs/dose-corrections.md DC-7/DC-8) for look and structure; the past-start question (lib/pastDoses.js, "Desde {date}, seriam {n} doses…") for the vial rule and the wording of the answers.
- journey review: 2026-11-03, separate-context journey-reviewer (pre-implementation, code only). Findings F1–F12 folded into the rows below (F1 → UP-3/UP-4, F2 → UP-5, F3 → UP-6, F4 → UP-18, F5 → UP-15/UP-16, F6 → UP-8 and UP-35, F7 → UP-22/UP-23, F8 → UP-36, F9 → UP-12, F10 → UP-29, F11 → UP-8, F12 → UP-17). Its product questions were answered by the founder on 2026-11-04 (end of this file).
- decided by logic in this draft (founder may change): one protocol per sheet in a queue; select → summary → Salvar; "Não tomei" and the unchosen doses of "Escolher quais" are written as Skipped; once a day, after other Today popups, three "Agora não" in a row → Today stops asking, the Dose log card stays; the vial counts only doses from the day that vial was mixed (or added); the oral bottle never changes; the lab trigger covers only charted protocols, only the doses that still count on the exam date, and only on accounts with the curve.
