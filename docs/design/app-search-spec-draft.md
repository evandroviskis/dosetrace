# App search: "Buscar no DoseTrace" (draft)

- source: founder idea 2026-11-03 (Second Brain dosetrace/02-decisoes.md, 2026-11-03 22:02 EST): a magnifier at the top right of Today; the person types what they are looking for ("perdidas", "doses") and the app suggests where it lives (missed, upcoming, pending doses, and the same for everything else in the app). Mainly for people who do not know the app yet: the founder spent 5 minutes looking for the missed doses, because the Dose log has two paths (Today › tracker › "Ver histórico", and My Protocols › "Registro de doses") and neither is called "missed".
- picture: docs/design/app-search.html (app today | proposal A | proposal B, light and dark, pt; a working demo of the matcher; the Fold book layout; the destination catalogue D01–D52 with synonyms in 6 languages; the new strings in 6 languages). Recommendation in the picture: A.
- proposals: **A** = the magnifier only on Today's header (top right). **B** = the same magnifier at the top right of all five tabs' headers (on My Protocols, left of "+ Adicionar"), with Shortcuts that follow the tab it was opened from. The search itself (field, shortcuts, results, no-result state, landing) is the same in both.
- decisions applied (ledger, not re-asked): the AI / no-advice hard line (CLAUDE.md, founder 2026-09-10): the search only navigates and never answers, recommends, diagnoses or interprets; analytics are anonymous and never carry search text (docs/decisions.md, founder answers 2026-10-04, "4 anônima"); Today's approved order (docs/decisions.md "Today", 2026-09-29) is unchanged under A; book layout rules BK-3 (Today's right page), BK-8 (selected outline), BK-10 / BK-14 (fold and unfold keep typed text), BK-15 (Android back), BK-22 (no back or close control on a right page) in docs/specs/book-layout.md; icons only from components/FeatureIcon.js (the "search" glyph already exists in assets/feature-icons/search.svg); no emoji; theme tokens only, both themes; natural translations in 6 languages, formal Portuguese, French "vous", es/de/it "tu/du" (founder 2026-10-02, 2026-10-20, 2026-10-31).
- decided by logic (founder may override; each goes into docs/decisions.md when signed):
  1. Offline, no AI, no network: a fixed catalogue of app places compiled into the app, with synonyms in all 6 languages. Reason: instant, free, works in airplane mode, and cannot drift into answering health questions.
  2. Matching ignores accents, case and punctuation; tolerates 1 wrong letter from 4 letters and 2 from 9; a half-typed word matches the start of a word; question words ("onde", "fica", "como", "where", "wo"…) are ignored; every remaining typed word must match. Synonyms of all 6 languages match at once; titles and paths show in the app language.
  3. A result opens the place through its normal path (switch to the path's tab, then open), already filtered or scrolled where that applies; the search closes. Reason: the person learns where things live, and Back behaves exactly as it does today.
  4. The search never acts: no save, change, delete, send or sign-out from a result; "Sair" and "Excluir conta" only scroll to and highlight their row.
  5. Places that do not exist right now are not listed (Pendente de ontem, Alertas, Encerrados, Excluídos recentemente); places that exist per protocol (Frasco, Calculadora, Horários e lembretes) are listed once per active protocol with its name; "Novo frasco" and "Editar frasco" need one of their own words, so "frasco" does not become nine rows.
  6. Nothing typed is stored (no recent searches) or sent anywhere.
  7. On the unfolded Fold the search opens as Today's right page, with no "Cancelar" there (BK-22).
- open founder questions (taste / scope only): (1) A or B; (2) should the search also find the person's own records by name, such as a lab marker ("TSH" › Lab test journal › TSH) or a vaccine ("gripe")? Protocol names are already in, because Frasco and Calculadora exist per protocol.
- planned code (not written): a catalogue module (lib/appSearch.js: destinations, synonyms, matcher), a search screen in MainStack, a new `filter` param on LogScreen (and on the book's Today right page), scroll / unfold targets on Today, ProtocolsScreen (`openProtocolId` + a block), ProgressScreen and SettingsScreen, the header button on TodayScreen (and the other tabs under B), new strings in i18n/translations.js x6.
- note on IDs: the rows use AS- (app search); SR- is already used by docs/specs/server-reminders.md (SR-1…SR-24).
