# App search: "Buscar no DoseTrace"

- source: founder idea 2026-11-03 (Second Brain dosetrace/02-decisoes.md, 2026-11-03 22:02 EST): a magnifier at the top right of Today; the person types what they are looking for ("perdidas", "doses") and the app suggests where it lives. Mainly for people who do not know the app yet: the founder spent 5 minutes looking for the missed doses, because the Dose log has two paths (Today › tracker › "Ver histórico", and My Protocols › "Registro de doses") and neither is called "missed".
- founder answer 2026-11-04 (verbatim): "Quanto a busca: Nao gostei de estar em lugares diferentes nas duas telas. Preciso da lupa no mesmo local em todas as telas ou somente na aba 'hoje'. E sim, a lupa deve ser um meio de o usuario encontrar qualquer coisa que ele nao saiba onde esta. Inclusive texto do privacy policy e etc". Applied here: proposal B (magnifier left of "+ Adicionar" on My Protocols) is rejected and replaced by B2; the search also covers the app's help and legal texts; the old open question 2 (own records by name) is answered "sim".
- picture: docs/design/app-search.html (app today | proposal A | proposal B2, light and dark, pt; the five tabs under A and under B2 with guide lines through the fixed point; the text results and the policy opened at the hit; own records; the Fold under A and B2; a working demo of the matcher with the real pt texts and sample records; the destination catalogue D01–D53, the text index and the record kinds; the new strings in 6 languages). Recommendation in the picture: B2 (the search now finds anything from anywhere, and the magnifier never moves); A remains the simpler option.
- proposals: **A** = the magnifier only on Today's header (top right, resting on the "Hoje" title line). **B2** = the same magnifier at one fixed point on all five tabs' headers: 8 pt below the status bar, 20 pt from the right edge, 44 pt; where a tab already has a control in that corner (only My Protocols: "+ Adicionar") the magnifier keeps the corner and the control sits immediately to its left; the Shortcuts follow the tab it was opened from. The search itself (field, shortcuts, results, text results, no-result state, landing) is the same in both.
- measured consequences of B2 (picture, 390 pt iPhone, SF Pro): on Today the magnifier sits 20 pt higher than under A (beside the date, because only Today has the date above its title); on My Protocols the title already wraps to two lines today in pt and de, and with the magnifier it also wraps in es, fr and it (en stays on one line; no word is cut); on Journey the subtitle gains a line in en, es and fr (pt and it already have two, de stays on one). My Body and Settings keep their layout.
- decisions applied (ledger, not re-asked): the AI / no-advice hard line (CLAUDE.md, founder 2026-09-10): the search only navigates and never answers, recommends, diagnoses or interprets; text results show the app's own fixed texts unchanged; analytics are anonymous and never carry search text (docs/decisions.md, founder answers 2026-10-04, "4 anônima"); Today's approved order (docs/decisions.md "Today", 2026-09-29) is unchanged under A and B2; book layout rules BK-3, BK-8, BK-10 / BK-14, BK-15, BK-22 in docs/specs/book-layout.md; icons only from components/FeatureIcon.js (the "search" glyph already exists in assets/feature-icons/search.svg); no emoji; theme tokens only, both themes; natural translations in 6 languages, formal Portuguese, French "vous", es/de/it "tu/du" (founder 2026-10-02, 2026-10-20, 2026-10-31); own records by name: "sim" (founder 2026-11-04); B rejected, magnifier in one place on every tab or only on Today (founder 2026-11-04).
- decided by logic (founder may override; each goes into docs/decisions.md when signed):
  1. Offline, no AI, no network: a fixed catalogue of app places compiled into the app, with synonyms in all 6 languages, plus the text index and the person's record names, all on the device. Reason: instant, free, works in airplane mode, and cannot drift into answering health questions.
  2. Place matching ignores accents, case and punctuation; tolerates 1 wrong letter from 4 letters and 2 from 9; a half-typed word matches the start of a word; question words ("onde", "fica", "como", "where", "wo"…) are ignored; every remaining typed word must match. Synonyms of all 6 languages match at once; titles and paths show in the app language.
  3. A result opens the place through its normal path (switch to the path's tab, then open), already filtered or scrolled where that applies; the search closes. Reason: the person learns where things live, and Back behaves exactly as it does today.
  4. The search never acts: no save, change, delete, send or sign-out from a result; "Sair" and "Excluir conta" only scroll to and highlight their row.
  5. Places that do not exist right now are not listed (Pendente de ontem, Alertas, Encerrados, Excluídos recentemente); places that exist per protocol (Frasco, Calculadora, Horários e lembretes) are listed once per active protocol with its name; "Novo frasco" and "Editar frasco" need one of their own words, so "frasco" does not become nine rows.
  6. Nothing typed is stored (no recent searches) or sent anywhere.
  7. On the unfolded Fold the search opens as the right page of the tab it was opened from, with no "Cancelar" there (BK-22).
  8. Texts are searched in the app language only (in six languages at once, short look-alike words such as "dado", "dato", "date" would bring unrelated paragraphs); places keep matching the six languages. The text index is built when the app starts from the same i18n/translations.js strings the screens show, so it can never differ from them.
  9. Record notes, values, units, dates and doses are not searched and never shown in a result (a result would otherwise put a health value out of its place).
  10. Screens opened from a tab (a protocol's page, the Lab test journal, the Dose log, Progress) keep their own back row and have no magnifier; under B2 the magnifier lives only on the five tab roots.
- founder pick 2026-11-04 15:10 EST (verbatim): "B2, pode aplicar e publicar". B2 is built; A is not. Where a row below still names A, only its B2 part applies.
- found while redrawing (fixed in the picture): the catalogue of 2026-11-03 had no entry for "Aviso médico" (Ajustes › Dados e privacidade › Aviso médico); added as D53.
- planned code (not written): a catalogue module (lib/appSearch.js: destinations, synonyms, matcher), a text index module (lib/appSearchText.js: builds the paragraphs from faq_categories, settings_privacy_body, settings_terms_body, settings_disclaimer_body and the LearnBlock explainers with lib/legalBlocks.js, matcher, excerpt and marks), a record source (protocol names and aliases, lab marker names via lib/markerNames.js, vaccine names) read from the local database, a search screen in MainStack, a `filter` param on LogScreen (and on the book's Today right page), scroll / unfold targets on Today, ProtocolsScreen (`openProtocolId` + a block), ProgressScreen and SettingsScreen, a `focus` param on components/LegalModal.js (scroll to a paragraph, mark words), an open-question param on FAQScreen, an open-item param on LearnBlock, open-marker / open-vaccine params on BodyScreen, the header button on TodayScreen (and under B2 one shared corner slot on the five tab roots), a new `mark` token in lib/theme.js (both palettes), new strings in i18n/translations.js x6.
- note on IDs: the rows use AS- (app search); SR- is already used by docs/specs/server-reminders.md (SR-1…SR-24).
