# Notifications blocked on the phone — a short sheet

- source: founder 2026-10-20 — "Pode seguir com as suas recomendações em todos"; docs/decisions.md 2026-10-20 "1.3.2 recommendations accepted: … a short sheet when notifications are blocked on the phone".
- purpose: when the phone blocks DoseTrace's notifications, no reminder can arrive — and nothing in the phone tells the person. Today already shows the "Seus lembretes estão bloqueados" card (RC-6, docs/specs/reminder-check.md); this adds one short DoseTrace sheet, once per state change, that says so plainly and opens the phone's settings. Never nagging.
- decided by logic (founder may override): "blocked on the phone" is the permission the phone reports as denied — never asked yet (undetermined) or unreadable is not blocked (DV-214); the sheet follows Today's card rule (dose reminders on, not Silent mode, a protocol with a reminder time: RC-6, BR-4 / AD-25); the button opens the same system screen as the Reminder check's notifications row (lib/notifications openReminderFix('notifications')); the state is kept on this phone (the permission belongs to the phone, not the account); the Android reminder setup screen (SP-5, shown once) goes first, since it lists Notifications too.
- never nagging (coordinator 2026-10-26, by the standing rules): "Não permitir" in a system dialog the app itself just showed (the shared AD-88 ask, onboarding "Never miss a dose") is the person's own choice, seen seconds ago — the answer is remembered as seen and the sheet never follows it. Only a later change counts: notifications turned off afterwards in the phone's settings, or still off at a later open after they had been on.
- no new visual pattern: the DoseTrace sheet (DTSheet: icon in a well circle, title, body, "Agora não" | primary), as Today's other sheets.
- code: lib/notifBlocked.js (the rule, the sheet, rememberDialogAnswer / askAndRemember), screens/TodayScreen.js (checkNotifBlocked, read with the reminder health), lib/notifications.js askNotificationPermission and screens/OnboardingFlowScreen.js enableNotifications (remember the dialog answer), i18n nb_* ×6.
