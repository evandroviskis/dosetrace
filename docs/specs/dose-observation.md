- decided by logic (founder may change):
  1. On the phone, a row of Today's "Doses concluídas" opens the Dose log with that dose's sheet (the picture draws the sheet over the Dose log); closing the sheet goes back to Today. On the open Fold the Dose log is already on the left page: the sheet opens there, Today stays on the right.
  2. While repeat is on, a change of the mark or the observation in one dose's sheet changes only that dose ("Num dia diferente, ela abre a dose e troca ou apaga"); the switch's line always says what is repeated. To repeat something else: switch off, then on in a dose that has the new mark / observation.
  3. Repeat applies to new completed doses only from the four entry points the picture names (Today Concluir and its later rows, the notification, "Mais tarde hoje", "Pendentes de ontem"); a dose completed later in the Dose log (a Missed or Skipped dose of another day) gets nothing automatically: the person describes it there.
  4. Restart as a new run ("Começar de novo") copies the repeat setting with the other settings (reminder time, notes): same routine.
  5. Saved text is the typed text without spaces / blank lines at the ends; empty = no observation.
