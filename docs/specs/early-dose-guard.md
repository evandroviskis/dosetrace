# Dose marked before its time: "Agora" and "Mais tarde hoje" on Today

- source: founder 2026-11-17 11:15 EST, real case: SLU-PP-332 at 08:00 and 20:00; at 08:10, after the 08:00 dose was marked, the card offered "Concluir" for the 20:00 dose and one tap recorded it 12 h early (he had to delete it in the history).
- picture: docs/design/early-dose-guard.html (claude.ai/artifact/L9ehnAKCL821vip9agwmHD), option B, notes "Como funcionaria (A e B)".
- checklist-signed: founder 2026-11-19 10:42 EST ("Siga. Aprovo tudo."): option B (Agora / Mais tarde hoje + the question when more than 2 h are left), limit 2 h. Second Brain 02-decisoes.md 2026-11-19 10:42 item (4).
- decided by logic (picture notes, how the features connect):
  1. The rule is one owner (lib/earlyDose.js): a dose is "early" when its scheduled time is MORE than 2 h after the tap; exactly 2 h or less, or a late dose, is not early. It is checked at the tap (handleTake), so every Today button that marks a dose of today follows it, not only the "Mais tarde hoje" list: a dose card, the "Skipped, you can still log it" line of a later slot, the caught-up card's skipped line, and My Protocols' "Mark complete" (A-42 opens Today and runs Today's own take).
  2. Order of questions: Pending from yesterday's "which day?" question stays first (unchanged; "Yesterday" writes yesterday's dose, never early); then the early question; then, for an injectable, the site question (S-25). Nothing is written before "Sim, concluí agora".
  3. "Sim, concluí agora" writes exactly what the card's Concluir writes for that slot: the slot named, at the real time of the "Sim" tap, the slot's reminders cancelled (ti + 1), the vial / bottle moved once, Undo offered. "Voltar", a tap outside or Android back write nothing.
  4. Pular is not offered in "Mais tarde hoje" (the picture shows only Concluir): skipping a dose hours before it is due is not something to resolve now, nothing is lost by waiting, and the dose moves up to "Agora" 2 h before its time with its Pular; its reminder still comes with its own buttons.
  5. Which block: each protocol has one card and the card offers its next open slot (A-78 cardPlan). The protocol is in "Mais tarde hoje" when that slot is early; otherwise in "Agora". A protocol without a reminder time has no time to compare and stays in "Agora". A card that still holds a loggable skipped slot of today stays whole in "Agora" (its skipped line is something to do now), and its main Concluir for the later slot asks.
  6. The labels: "Agora" with its count only when "Mais tarde hoje" also has rows (with no later dose the cards look exactly as before); "Mais tarde hoje" with its count whenever it has rows. The section title keeps "Doses N de M concluídas". The caught-up card ("all done") still needs every dose of today taken or skipped, so a later dose keeps it away.
  7. The screen follows the clock while open: Today already re-renders every minute while focused and on return to the foreground (useClock everyMinute, DV-148), so at 18:00 the 20:00 dose moves up by itself; the rule is evaluated again at the tap.
  8. The dose from a reminder (A-44 "From your reminder") stays highlighted at the top, outside both blocks; its take is the reminder's slot (at its time, never early in practice) and follows the same rule. The notification's own "Concluir" (lib/notificationActions.js) never asks: it arrives at the dose time.
  9. Pending from yesterday, Doses concluídas, Tomorrow / Next 5 days: unchanged.
  10. App search: the "Doses" result still lands on the doses block, which holds both "Agora" and "Mais tarde hoje"; no new search entry.
  11. The take animation (the drop to the rings): a direct Concluir keeps it; after the question there is no drop flight (the sheet covered the button, as after the site question, S-25); the ring and the count still move to the new value.
  12. Accessibility: both labels are headers; a later row reads "name, time, dose, in 11 h 50 min" and opens the protocol; its button reads "Concluir, name, time"; the question is the app sheet (title as header, two buttons).
  13. Other places that mark a dose: the Dose log only changes existing rows (Missed / Skipped / no dose) through a sheet with choices, and the manual form takes a time the person types; both are deliberate and stay as they are. The protocol page (A-115) has no mark buttons.
  14. Phone and Fold: one column keeps the FT-6 order, the doses block is now Agora + Mais tarde hoje; on the open Fold the doses block is on the RIGHT page (FT-2), so both blocks live there; a later row opens its protocol page on the left (FT-3) with the outline on the row.
- committed-to: next delivery (EAS Update, JS only).
