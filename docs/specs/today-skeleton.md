## What the picture decides (summary)
- Today appears at once with the date, "Hoje", the tab bar and the cards at their final size in a quiet tone, without any number or text; each card fills in when its own data arrives, in the same place.
- The cards keep their real colour (c.raised, c.slab) so nothing flashes when the data arrives. Bars: c.well inside the white / grey cards, c.slabInv.line inside the ring block, c.line on the ground. No new token.
- The shapes come from the last opening's "mould": which cards were shown (alerts, ring, food log, how many doses) and each one's height, kept on the device, never data. First opening (or after a reinstall): ring, food log, 2 doses, no alerts.
- The only movement allowed: if the day changed since the last opening (an alert appeared or went away), that block adjusts once, in place. Never "Nenhum registro ainda" nor "Adicione um protocolo" before the read finished.
- The full-screen circle also goes: if the last session was signed in, the app opens straight on Today's skeleton. Without a session it stays as it is (welcome / sign in).
- Pulse on: a slow 1.6 s pulse; always still with "Remover animações" (Android) / "Reduzir movimento" (iPhone).
- The skeleton does not make the data arrive faster; Today is complete at the same moment as before. The 4 s cap of A-120 stays. Screen reader: "Carregando" once, the bars hidden. Outer screen and inner screen (book): the same skeleton in Today's centred column.
