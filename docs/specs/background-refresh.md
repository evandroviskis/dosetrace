# Reminders without opening the app (Android background refresh) — A-107

- source: founder 2026-10-07 — option B ("B, e o A-106 entra na 1.3.0"); the principle "o app avisa até a pessoa entrar no aplicativo e deletar esse protocolo… não posso deixar o Android definir" (docs/decisions.md 2026-10-07). Journey review of the plan (separate agent, 2026-10-07) → AC1-AC11 below.
- purpose: reminders are scheduled on the phone for a window (doses 21 days, morning summary 7 days, the 20:00 food question while a check is open) and were only renewed when the app was opened. A user who keeps taking something without opening the app must keep getting its reminders until they delete the protocol.
- design: an Android background task (expo-background-task, every 6 h, Android decides the moment) runs the same resync the app runs, in background mode. Scheduling never cancels first: it schedules what it wants (fixed ids replace) and only then cancels what is no longer wanted; an unknown state cancels nothing. iOS unchanged (no background mode, by an earlier decision).
- code: lib/reminderRefresh.js (rules), lib/backgroundTasks.js (wiring), lib/notifications.js (non-destructive syncs, background mode, one run at a time), lib/supabase.js (stored session read), index.js / App.js (define / register), package.json (iOS autolinking exclude).
- checklist-signed: 2026-10-07 by the founder ("assino as duas")
- committed-to: 1.3.0 (approved → next build)
