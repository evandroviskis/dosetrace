## What the picture decides (summary)
- **B** (Edit profile and sign-up About you): the help line under the field stays as it is ("Used for calorie & energy estimates — choose the sex you were assigned at birth."); under it a "Why do we ask?" link with a small chevron opens the full text in a well box: a bold line "Why we ask for sex at birth", then two paragraphs. A tap again closes it.
- **C** (Edit profile; sign-up only when the account already has a saved value): when an already-saved value is changed, an outlined note with an info glyph appears between the bar and the help line: "What this changes" + the short text. It goes away when the choice is back to the saved value; nothing is saved until Save (Cancel undoes). Never on a first choice.
- **E** (Progress › Your numbers): the field uses the profile's own terms ("Sex at birth", Male / Female exactly as in Edit profile, not the old "Sex" with Man / Woman) and the same "Why do we ask?" link, full width under the Age / Sex row, opening the same text.
- **D′** (Your numbers, where a tap saves at once): changing a saved value first opens the DoseTrace sheet "Change to {value}?" with the short text and Cancel / Change; only Change saves. A first choice saves at once.
- **Wording** (D′ adjustment): the 200–300 kcal claim is only made "if body fat is not entered" (with body fat the app uses Katch-McArdle, which does not use sex). True reasons only: energy formulas, the minimum calories in the goals, the injection-site figure. The Dose accumulation curve does not use sex (decision 2026-09-09) and the text says so. Neutral, no judgement of identity; hormone therapy → the user's health professional.
