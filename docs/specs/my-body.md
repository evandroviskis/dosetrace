# My Body — the Graduated redesign (hub, Lab test journal, Vaccine journal, popups)

- source: founder decision 2026-10-03, "siga as recomendações no My Body", approved from the picture page https://claude.ai/artifact/248DHnSfvXWrDi62bXmXP5 (20 parts; prototype | proposal | app today, light and dark). Every part follows the binding prototype (docs/design/prototype.html, the "Sam Carter" version) except parts 5, 6, 7 and 9, which take the proposal copy: the S-24 Premium wording with no number in the Premium lines, "Scan service unavailable" without "or enter values manually", and the monthly-limit message that shows the server's limit (A-60). The scan NUMBER appears only in the limit message (parts 5, 6, 7 consistent).
- gap list closed: the My Body audit of 2026-10-01 (hub hero cards, starred markers, Next due, Dose accumulation number, icons, Latest caption, hero weight 300, Export hidden when empty, native alerts → DoseTrace sheets, Cancel before Go Premium, bottom sheets that hug content, date-wheel sheets, vaccine-name toast, one spinner, retired review sheets).
- founder decisions that apply everywhere: palette B (lib/protocolColors displayColor), SegmentedBar for side-by-side choices, DoseTrace sheets instead of native alerts, every delete asks first.
- code: screens/BodyScreen.js (hub, Lab test journal, test, marker, sheets), screens/components/VaccinesSection.js (Vaccine journal, add/edit, scan), screens/components/BodySheets.js (bottom sheet, sheet bars, toast), lib/bodyHub.js, lib/bodyScan.js, lib/bodyDates.js (pure rules), components/featureIconsData.js (lab_frame, syringe_tilt).
- data: nothing is migrated or dropped. Lab values, vaccines, favourites and labels stay where they are; every delete is the synced tombstone (sync_status 'deleted').
