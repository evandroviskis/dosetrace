- source: founder decision 2026-10-03, "siga as recomendações no My Body", approved from the picture page https://claude.ai/artifact/248DHnSfvXWrDi62bXmXP5 (20 parts; prototype | proposal | app today, light and dark). - data: nothing is migrated or dropped. Lab values, vaccines, favourites and labels stay where they are; every delete is the synced tombstone (sync_status 'deleted').
