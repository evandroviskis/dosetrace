# Make sure your reminders arrive — as a guided conversation

- source: founder 2026-11-05, inspired by the Lemonade app's permission chat ("como se fosse uma conversa… abre a configuração do Android, já exatamente onde tem que clicar… quando volta para a tela… agora eu preciso do próximo passo"); then "A, pode escrever ao Google e desenhar a conversa"; then on the picture: "1 sim, 2 ícone, 3 Deixa conforme a sugestão que você montou aqui por enquanto. Enquanto a gente espera a resposta do Google."
- picture (binding): docs/design/reminder-setup-conversation.html (claude.ai/artifact/W1H6JuuS5RzAnsg6AkM1C5), light and dark. Battery = version B (the app's own Android page + "Bateria › Sem restrições"); version A (the system "always run in the background" dialog) is NOT built: Google's first answer was no (support ticket 2026-11-05, human answer pending).
- why: the list shows every phone setting at once and the person must understand each alone. The conversation takes one setting at a time, says why it matters, opens the exact Android screen, reads the phone again on return, confirms, and goes to the next.
- replaces (rebuild = replace): the list of components/ReminderSetupList.js inside screens/ReminderCheckScreen.js (Android) and inside the Android onboarding step of screens/OnboardingFlowScreen.js. Every door that opened the list opens the conversation: Settings › Notifications first row, the Today reminder cards, the notification tap, the once-per-phone automatic opening (SP-5), the onboarding step. The decided rules of docs/specs/reminder-setup-placement.md (A-112/A-113), reminder-check.md and notifications-blocked.md stay true unless a row below says otherwise: what is read, what counts, when it opens, the Settings row states, the Today card rules.
- no AI and no network: every sentence is fixed app text in the 6 languages; no model call; works offline. No fake attendant: the DoseTrace mark sits beside the first message of each step only (founder "2 ícone").
- checklist-signed: 2026-11-05, founder ("1 sim, 2 ícone, 3 …por enquanto", on the picture)
- committed-to: the next delivery (JS only: EAS Update on the store channel after the founder tests it)
