# Food: plan beside what was logged (food goals design, parts 1, 2 and 3)

- source: founder 2026-11-18 20:40 EST ("Pode construir o resto"), on docs/design/food-goals-progress.html. His reason: "um tracker de comida que, na verdade, não dá feedback nenhum… só coloca um número na tela e o usuário que se vire para saber o quanto deveria estar consumindo… perdeu um pouco do sentido se a gente não puder direcionar a pessoa para que lado ela está indo" — shallow direction only: the plan beside what was logged, never what to eat. This REPLACES the 2026-11-08 limit "one line, not a planning screen" (docs/specs/food-fiber-week.md FW-8 stays as built; the charts now live in Progress).
- picture: docs/design/food-goals-progress.html parts 1 (Progress › Your daily plan + Plan and actual), 2 (food chat after a log) and 3 (food card on Today and Journey) (claude.ai/artifact/6t3vCHEhC4URggQv72YEUZ).
- code: lib/planActual.js (the one math: plan targets, today's bars, the bar text, the 7/14-day chart, the counts), lib/progressTile.js (plan), lib/progressTileLoad.js (the one loader), lib/progressCard.js (layout), screens/components/PlanBars.js, screens/components/PlanActualCard.js, screens/components/CalculatorSection.js, screens/FoodChatScreen.js, screens/components/FoodLogHero.js, screens/JourneyScreen.js, i18n x6.
- tests: __tests__/foodPlanActual.test.js
- checklist-signed: founder 2026-11-18 20:40 EST ("Pode construir o resto")
- decided by logic:
  - Fat: the plan has a fat target, but the food log does not total fat (a food_logs row carries kcal, protein_g and carb_g; lib/nutrition dayTotals sums only those) and the picture says "Gordura fica só no plano". So no fat bar; fat stays in the plan chip.
  - Fiber: there is no fiber target (FW-1: a number only, no target, founder 2026-11-08). So no fiber bar; in the chat the day's fiber stays as a number under the bars when known (FW-9 kept).
  - The plan is the Progress card's own numbers for the chosen goal: calories = the goal tile (rounded to 10; off the measured burn through the floored engine once a reality check exists), protein = the card's protein (rounded to 5), carbs = what the plan leaves for carbs at that goal's calories (rounded to 5). Found while building and fixed: the card's "fat · carbs" chip came from the formula goal even when a reality check had measured the burn, while the tiles above it showed the measured goal; the chip now reads the same plan, so chip, bars and charts show one number.
  - The plan line in the charts is today's plan: the app keeps no history of past plans.
  - A day "with no log" = no food read that day (the same rule as the chat's day total, lib/foodThread dayFoodTotal); a day closed as "I ate nothing" has no food read either and stays empty.
  - Today's column is drawn faded, with no shortfall outline (the day is not over), and is left out of the counts.
  - The chart's top is the larger of 1.5 x the plan and the highest day, so no day is cut off.
  - 14 days is the default (the picture); the choice is kept on the device (a convenience, not user data).
  - "+N g protein in your day" goes on the latest of today's entries only (the one just logged; an earlier day is not "your day"); protein because it is the plan card's headline number and the picture names it.
  - The Today / Journey card without a plan keeps its day line as before; the chat keeps the numbers and adds "Set up your plan in Progress to compare". The Journey week block (FW-6/FW-8) is unchanged.
  - "Log food ›" in Progress opens the food chat (the chat shows its own lock when logging is locked).
- hard line: numbers side by side only. Never "eat more/less", never a food suggestion, never red, good or bad, never a cause ("why you did not lose weight"). Above plan is grey (ink3), never risk/attention.
- committed-to: next delivery (EAS Update, JS only)
