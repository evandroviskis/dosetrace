# Weight and body fat: lean mass, and the measured burn redone with body fat (food goals design, part 5)

- source: founder 2026-11-17 (weight going up while eating below plan: the user must never think the app is wrong); design docs/design/food-goals-progress.html part 5 ("Peso e gordura: quando o peso sobe e a gordura cai"); decision 2026-11-19 10:42 EST in Second Brain/dosetrace/02-decisoes.md item (2): body fat enters the math only with at least 2 body-fat readings at the START and 2 at the END of the Reality check ("Seus números reais") measurement; otherwise everything stays as today (weight only) and the screen says which math was used.
- picture: docs/design/food-goals-progress.html part 5 (claude.ai/artifact/6t3vCHEhC4URggQv72YEUZ).
- checklist-signed: founder 2026-11-19 10:42 EST ("Siga. Aprovo tudo.")
- decided by logic:
  - Fat readings = weigh-ins (calc_snapshots) holding both a weight and a body fat % (above 0, below 100), one per day (the last row of a day wins, as lib/weightTrend). Fat kg = weight × % ÷ 100; lean mass = weight − fat kg.
  - Table "Today | since {date}": the first column is the latest fat reading (its weight, fat kg · %, lean); its header reads "Today" when that reading is today, else its date. The change column compares two AVERAGES with the same 7-day window as lib/weightTrend and the founder's minimum of 2 readings: the readings in the 7 days that end on the latest reading, minus the readings in the 7 days that start on the first reading; "since {date}" is that first reading. Both windows need 2 readings and must not overlap; otherwise there is no change column. The weight row of the table is the weight of those same readings, so weight change = fat change + lean change (as in the picture). The existing "Since {date}" line (first and last weigh-in, waist) stays as it is.
  - Measured burn with body fat: the start readings are those in the first 7 days of the check (start date to start + 6), the end readings those in the 7 days that end on the finishing weigh-in (end − 6 to end), the same 7-day window. With at least 2 in each: Δfat = mean fat kg at the end − at the start, Δlean likewise; energy change = Δfat × 9,400 + Δlean × 1,800 kcal (Hall KD, Int J Obes 2008); the days are the span between the mean dates of the two groups (the change happened between those dates: precision principle, founder 2026-10-26); burn = the check's mean food kcal per day − energy change ÷ days. A span under 7 days (not reachable with a 21-day check) uses weight only.
  - Plausibility: the with-fat result keeps the existing 1,000–5,000 kcal range. Outside it, the weight-only math is used (as today) and the line says why; a weight-only result outside the range with a with-fat result inside it gets the with-fat result. A check with no food or under 14 days stays "no result", as today.
  - Precision principle (founder 2026-10-26): when the rule holds, the with-fat burn IS the measured burn. Today the measured burn flows from one place: completing the check writes reality_checks.tdee, and the hero, "Your daily plan" (goalsForTdee), the plan bars, the target projection, the Journey tile and the food card all read the latest reality_checks.tdee. So the with-fat number is what is written there; the weight-only number is kept only for the comparison row (smaller). Today's alert (lib/realityCheckNow) runs the same function, so it says what Progress shows.
  - The math used is saved with the result in the synced calc_inputs payload as rcResultMath { [weigh-in date]: { method, weightOnly, withFat, startN, endN, days, avgKcal, reason } }, merged per date and never clobbered (like rcResultSources), because the check's start, readings and food average are gone once it closes. A result saved before this version has no record: its view is exactly as before.
  - "Why the math changes so much" is shown only under a with-fat result; the design's caption sentence ("o senhor gastou mais do que comeu") is an example for the founder, not app copy.
  - The hint sits under the weight / body fat row of "Log today's weight" (the sheet the picture names).
- hard line: the user's own numbers and general facts with a source only. Never "good"/"bad", "don't worry", "it's normal", "it's water", "you gained muscle", "you lost fat", "trust the process", "the app is right"; no advice; no compound named; no reading of what the numbers mean for the person.
- committed-to: next delivery (EAS Update, JS only).
