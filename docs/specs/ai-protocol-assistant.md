# AI protocol assistant

- source: founder conversation 2026-10-02. Visual proposal: https://claude.ai/artifact/9xEU7h9H1FnUx4TgKUZnhE (four doors) and https://claude.ai/artifact/WHoE7BRXggmpgCjCMkhw85 (animated "why it doesn't fit" explainer).
- purpose: an OPTIONAL help next to the manual form, which stays the default. Lay users get stuck filling in a protocol. A real customer typed her weekly total (240 mg) as the dose per injection and got a "doesn't fit the syringe" error she could not understand. The assistant lets anyone build or finish a protocol by answering simple questions in their own words. It only asks, understands and transcribes; the app does every calculation from numbers the user gave. It NEVER suggests or questions a compound, dose, water amount or schedule (CLAUDE.md AI hard line; founder 2026-10-02: "todos os números que saem da calculadora sejam fornecidos pelo cliente. A gente nunca pode sugerir nada.").
- cost: approved by the founder 2026-10-02 ("eu aprovo sim qualquer custo").
- checklist-signed: 2026-10-02 by the founder (in chat: "H. Assinado"), with his answers d–g applied. "Plan my mix" (former AP-24) dropped by the founder 2026-10-02: the app cannot know how much liquid a vial holds, so a water amount to reach a chosen number of units could tell a lay user to add more water than the vial can take
- committed-to: next build (founder "approved → next build" rule)
