# Premium (paywall) and Onboarding + Auth — the Graduated redesign

- source: founder decision 2026-10-03, "1 sim" (docs/decisions.md, "Founder answers 2026-10-03"), approved from the picture pages proto/pay2/premium.html (6 parts) and proto/onb2/entrada.html (12 parts): prototype | proposal | app today, light and dark. Everything follows the binding prototype (docs/design/prototype.html, the "Sam Carter" version) with the resolved points below.
- resolved points (docs/decisions.md, no question to the founder): real store prices from RevenueCat at runtime, never hard-coded, and any saving computed from them (no fixed "Save 37%"); the S-24 table (Premium scans = a check, "Lab, vaccine & vial scans — photo or PDF, any language"); AI food log free = 7 days (FREE_DAYS); previews never interpretive (AI hard line); every paywall and auth message a DoseTrace sheet; Apple 3.1.2 (billed price biggest, trial length + later price on the button, Restore, Terms (EULA), Privacy); the welcome logo is the droplet (decided 2026-09-07), not the prototype's blue square; the Google button per Google's branding guidelines (updated 2026-07-07: Google Sans Medium, current G colours, 12 pt gap on iOS, light/dark per theme, "Continue with Google"); the Apple button stays the system button; 5 activity levels with existing users' choice migrated; country stays required; "Finish setup" stays for Apple/Google sign-ups with missing fields (build-49 gate); the consent screen's AI line says the app reads, never analyses; Terms of service linked as well as Privacy policy.
- bugs fixed test-first: ResetPasswordScreen hard-coded English "OK" and raw server errors; Back from Create account restarted sign-up with empty fields; the consent row hidden from sign-up / only Privacy linked; native alerts on the paywall.
- journey review (2026-10-03, before building): gaps folded in as PA-12 (pending purchase), PA-16 (already Premium), PA-6 (retry, no button before eligibility), PA-41 (Finish setup never blanks hidden fields, refreshes, prefills a fresh account), PA-37 (legacy activity kept), PA-53 (stay on Create, Apple/Google named), PA-56 (honest reset wording), PA-58 (resend on not-confirmed), PA-59 (social sign-in honours the consent box), PA-63/PA-39 (Terms in both consent places), PA-66 (failed email links explained). Open founder decisions are listed at the end.
- code: screens/PaywallScreen.js, screens/AuthScreen.js, screens/OnboardingFlowScreen.js, screens/ResetPasswordScreen.js, App.js (routing + email links), screens/SettingsScreen.js (Edit profile activity), lib/paywallPlans.js, lib/authFlow.js, lib/onboardingSteps.js, lib/activityLevels.js (pure rules), lib/purchases.js (pending purchase), lib/fonts.js + assets/fonts/ (Google Sans Medium, OFL), components/featureIconsData.js (check, alert).
- data: nothing is dropped. The onboarding answers stay in the device stash until the account has them; the activity level is migrated on read (old key kept as activity_level_legacy when rewritten).
- checklist-signed: pending — design approved from pictures 2026-10-03
- committed-to: next build (founder "approved → next build" rule)

## Open founder decisions (journey review 2026-10-03; nothing built for them)
1. Shared device: a person who finishes onboarding but never creates an account leaves their answers on the phone; the next person to create an email account there gets them (consent is no longer pre-ticked for them). Show "whose answers" on Create account with "Not you? Start over"? (needs a picture first).
2. Whether onboarding answers may fill an EXISTING account's missing fields (today: only accounts created in the last hour, the leak guard).
3. A subscriber buying Lifetime from the paywall (today the already-Premium sheet stops it); RevenueCat's restore/transfer setting when one Apple ID is used by two DoseTrace accounts.
4. The 18+ check uses the year only (a 17-year-old born later this year passes); what to do with accounts created under the old 13+ floor.
5. An Apple-created account on Android (there is no Apple button on Android).
6. A reset link opened while signed in as another account switches accounts silently; killing the app on Reset password lands in the app without a new password.
7. Confirm in Play Console that the free-trial offer is "new customers only", so the Android trial claim matches who gets it.
